## Full Description (App Store & Google Play)

DualisCheckUp is a wellness and self-awareness app. It helps you keep a daily journal of how you feel physically and emotionally, track habits such as hydration and sleep, view your own entries over time, and access general educational content about healthy habits.

### What you can do with Dualis:

- **1-Minute Daily Check-in:** Easily record how your body and mind feel today in a simple, guided daily flow.
- **Physical & Emotional Journal:** Keep track of personal physical comfort and emotional states over time with interactive body maps and trend graphs.
- **Pattern Insights:** Discover helpful patterns and recurring tendencies in your self-reported logs to better understand your daily rhythms.
- **Habit Tracking:** Monitor hydration, sleep quality, and healthy daily routines.
- **Wellness Readings:** General educational content regarding sleep hygiene, posture, nutrition, stress management, and active lifestyles, citing verifiable public health sources.
- **Always-Accessible Safety Resources:** Direct, one-tap access to emergency telephone numbers and crisis lines from any screen at any time, without barriers or algorithmic gates.
- **Data Privacy & Security:** Your sensitive personal data is protected with end-to-end encryption and strict data sovereignty controls.

### Important Notice:
DualisCheckUp is not a medical device: it does not assess, classify or interpret symptoms, does not indicate urgency levels, does not diagnose, does not recommend treatment and does not replace a healthcare professional. In an emergency, call your local emergency number. If you are in emotional distress, contact your local crisis line.

## Privacy Nutrition Label Notes (Apple App Store)

- **Data Collected:**
  - **Health & Fitness (Wellness Journal Data):** Self-reported physical sensations, mood entries, and habit statistics provided voluntarily by the user. Purpose: App functionality.
  - **Identifiers (User ID, Email):** For account creation, secure sign-in, and profile management.
  - **Diagnostics:** Non-identifiable crash logs and performance latency metrics for technical reliability.
- **Third-Party Tracking:** No cross-app or cross-site tracking.
- **Advertising:** Native contextual ads served based strictly on coarse age bracket and approximate location for local businesses. Health and wellness data is NEVER shared with advertisers (compliant with Apple 5.1.3).
- **Security:** AES-256 field encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, and database Row-Level Security (RLS) isolation.
