# Journal v2.6: which everyday factors go with each body option

Goal: the triggers screen ("Tem algo que pode ter contribuído hoje?") of a physical entry only offers factors that people commonly connect with what they marked. It never says a factor caused anything; it only narrows the list. Sources are general health-education knowledge (patient leaflets, NHS / Mayo Clinic / MSD Manual consumer pages, well-known reviews). Where the link is folk belief only (cold air and a stiff neck, humidity and back pain) it was left out; "Outro" and "Não sei" are always there.

Trigger keys: trabalho (Trabalho ou estudos), familia (Família ou relações), sono (Noite mal dormida), dinheiro (Dinheiro), telas (Telas ou redes sociais), ciclo (Ciclo menstrual), alimentacao (Alimentação), esforco (Esforço físico), queda (Queda ou mau jeito), medicamento (Medicamento novo), clima (Clima ou ambiente).

"Stress" = trabalho + familia + dinheiro. They go together: the app has no way to tell which source of stress fits, so it offers all three where stress is a common, documented contributor, and none where it is not.
