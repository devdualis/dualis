# Plan 06-01: Somatic-Emotional Clinical Matrix Engine, Organic Primacy Protocol, and Triage Outcome Backend Service

**Phase:** 6 — Triage Outcome, Somatic Mapping & Article Recommendations (Screen 6 / RF-004 & RF-005)
**Plan:** 06-01
**Requirements covered:** SOM-01, SOM-02, OUT-01, REC-01
**Mode:** mvp

---

## Goal

Implement the NestJS clinical outcome determination engine:
1. Normalize user-reported symptoms across 19 categories (7 emotional dimensions and 12 physical systems).
2. Deterministically apply the **Organic Primacy** protocol (`SOM-02`).
3. Compute calculated intensity score (1–5) and care disposition (`Auto-cuidado`, `Consulta de Rotina`, `Pronto Atendimento`, `Emergência`).
4. Provide a repository of curated evidence-based medical articles authored by clinical specialists (`REC-01`).
5. Expose `POST /v1/triage/outcome` authenticated endpoint that saves encrypted symptom logs in PostgreSQL (`symptom_logs` under RLS) and returns the full outcome payload.
