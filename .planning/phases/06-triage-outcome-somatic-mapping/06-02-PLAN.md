# Plan 06-02: Screen 6 Triage Outcome UI, Dimension Badges, Disposition Gauge & Curated Article Carousel

**Phase:** 6 — Triage Outcome, Somatic Mapping & Article Recommendations (Screen 6 / RF-004 & RF-005)
**Plan:** 06-02
**Requirements covered:** SOM-01, SOM-02, OUT-01, REC-01, I18N-01
**Mode:** mvp

---

## Goal

Implement Screen 6: Triage Outcome & Recommendations in Flutter:
1. Display the calculated intensity score (1 to 5) with a chromatic severity meter/gauge.
2. Render the primary clinical category badge with lay-term-to-clinical normalization label.
3. Render the Organic Primacy clinical notice banner when physical symptoms are present alongside emotional distress.
4. Render the Care Disposition card with clear actionable guidance (`Auto-cuidado`, `Consulta de Rotina`, `Pronto Atendimento`, `Emergência`).
5. Render the Curated Preventive Articles carousel / cards authored by clinical specialists with reading time and external/modal links.
6. Connect `TriageWizardScreen` (Screen 4) Step 5 submission to `/triage-outcome` and provide an exit CTA returning to Home.
7. Trilingual localization in Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`), Spanish (`es`), and English (`en`).
